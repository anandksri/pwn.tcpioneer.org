# PWN.TCPIONEER Design System

## Brand
PWN.TCPIONEER

## Core palette
- Primary: `#8B5CF6`
- Supporting violet: `#7C3AED`, `#A78BFA`
- Background: `#09090B`
- Surface: `#0F0F11`
- Secondary surface: `#18181B`
- Border: `#27272A`
- Primary text: `#FAFAFA`
- Secondary text: `#A1A1AA`
- Muted text: `#71717A`
- Success: `#22C55E`
- Warning: `#F59E0B`
- Danger: `#EF4444`
- Info: `#3B82F6`

## Radius
- Default radius: `0px`
- Square corners are preferred across cards, panels, navigation, inputs, dropdowns, and modals.
- Rounded shapes are reserved for semantic elements such as avatars, notification dots, and compact badges.

## Typography
- Primary UI: Geist / Inter
- Technical content: JetBrains Mono
- Headings should be strong, compact, and readable without oversized decorative scaling.

## Design philosophy
- Minimal
- Developer-focused
- Cybersecurity-oriented
- Professional
- Flat surfaces
- High contrast
- Accessible
- No excessive decoration

## Components
### Buttons
- Simple, compact, and consistent
- Minimal border and clear hover states
- Primary buttons use violet accent without gradient fills
- Secondary buttons keep neutral surfaces with violet hover emphasis

### Cards
- Use flat surfaces with subtle borders
- Default background is near-black or zinc-950
- Hover states are subtle and color-based rather than elevated shadows

### Inputs
- Border-based, low-noise developer search and form controls
- Clear focus states with violet accent
- Minimal radius and consistent sizing

### Navigation
- Sidebar items favor a soft active state with a violet accent bar or border
- No large pill backgrounds or floating nav shapes

### Sidebar
- Expanded width: `280px`
- Collapsed width: `88px`
- Toggle button lives inside the sidebar itself
- Active item uses a compact accent treatment and stronger contrast

### Badges
- Compact and technical
- Use semantic colors only when they convey status or emphasis

### Tables and dialogs
- Clean borders, strong type, and low-noise surfaces
- Keep motion subtle and purposeful

## States
- Default: dark neutral surfaces
- Hover: slightly lighter neutral backgrounds
- Active: violet accent and stronger contrast
- Focus: clear visible outline or ring using the violet accent

## Overall impression
The UI should feel like a serious cyber learning platform: dark, professional, dense but readable, and technical without turning into a generic SaaS dashboard or a neon-heavy mockup.
